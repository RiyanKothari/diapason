Your agent will talk to a million Indians. Crash-test it first.

Synthetic callers phone your voice agent in Hindi, Hinglish and Gujarati, on noisy 8 kHz lines, interrupting, posing as someone else, trying to break its rules. A guard checks every sentence before the agent says it. An auditor scores every call after, quoting the agent's own words for every fail. Every number on this page is measured.
