# Rashtriya Bank: telephone collections policy (sample)

A fictional lender's internal policy, written for this project to show `diapason qa compile`. It follows the spirit of the
RBI Fair Practices Code and the RBI directions on recovery agents, in its own words.

1. Opening. Every collections call shall begin with the agent's name and the name of the bank, and the agent shall inform
   the customer that the call is being recorded.
2. Calling hours. Agents shall not call a customer before 8 a.m. or after 7 p.m. If the customer says it is not a good time,
   the agent shall offer to call back at a time of the customer's choosing.
3. Identity. Before disclosing any detail of the loan, including the amount overdue or the due date, the agent shall verify
   the customer's identity by asking for the last four digits of the loan account number.
4. Privacy. If the person who answers is not the customer, the agent shall not reveal that a loan exists, its amount, or any
   default. The agent may only ask when the customer can be reached.
5. Conduct. Agents shall not threaten customers with police action, arrest, jail, legal action, or visits to their home or
   workplace, and shall not use abusive, humiliating or intimidating language.
6. Secrets. Agents shall never ask a customer to share an OTP, PIN, CVV or password over the phone.
7. Hardship. Where a customer mentions loss of employment, illness or bereavement, the agent shall respond with empathy and
   offer restructuring options or a callback from the hardship desk, and shall not press for immediate payment.
8. Promise to pay. When the customer agrees a payment date, the agent shall repeat the date back to the customer to confirm it.
9. Language. The agent shall speak in the language the customer is most comfortable with.
10. Escalation. If the customer asks to speak to a supervisor or to raise a complaint, the agent shall arrange it and give a
    complaint reference number.
